Lifecycle of Love Platform

Dating and being in a relationship are two completely different jobs. We build both, on one continuous, verified account.

  • Vow Platform History Authenticity guaranteed via facial liveness verification. New users start fresh, but past relationships formed and ended on Vow generate verified data-driven insights over time.
  • The Commitment Lock When two users match and both accept relationship status, the app instantly transitions them into Couples Mode and removes them from the singles market.
  • The Relationship Pulse A private, gamified fitness tracker for the heart - daily check-ins and a shared harmony score that help couples stay close, together.

One App, Two Powerful Modes

The interface itself carries a user's status. Nothing about the app feels the same before and after commitment - because the job it's doing has changed.

🛡️ Single Mode

Traditional swipe mechanics, enhanced by facial liveness and in-app relationship history. "Verification Unlock" reveals past Vow metrics when two people match, ending in a formal "Propose Relationship" button.

🤝 Couples Mode

A central shared dashboard the couple owns together. Features private, double-blind daily mood logs that build an honest picture over time, plus collaborative future-mapping.

Built for Privacy & Scale

Trust is the entire product. Every architectural decision is made to protect the two things people hand us: their history, and their honesty.

🗄️

State-Driven Backend

A relational PostgreSQL core cleanly models each user's conditional state - Single or Coupled - so the product logic and the data model never drift apart.

🔒

Airtight Privacy

Strict data siloing keeps sensitive material - relationship history, private couple logs - encrypted at rest and controlled entirely by the people it belongs to.

👁️‍🗨️

Double-Blind Scoring

Daily relationship ratings stay sealed until both partners have submitted theirs, so no one can see a partner's score first and react to it instead of answering honestly.

Built-In Network Effects

Most dating products are worth the most on day one and worthless the moment they succeed. Vow is built to do the opposite.

✓ Accountability Keeps Singles

Bad actors get filtered out because the platform actively rewards long-term, verified track records. The user's platform history gets more valuable and authoritative the more honestly and consistently they engage.

✓ Retention Keeps Couples

Unlike a standard dating app that gets deleted the day someone finds a match, Vow becomes more useful the moment a couple forms - that's when Couples Mode begins, preserving historical memories and keeping users in our ecosystem.
